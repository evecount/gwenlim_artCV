## 🏛️ STUDIO OVERVIEW
A full-suite visual production agency and decentralized commercial studio network operating across high-compliance sectors, from luxury automotive and global enterprise to grassroots mutual aid. Motion and Still Inc. engineered dynamic, scalable visual architecture, bridging the gap between highly structured corporate campaigns, nimble startup ecosystems, and community-driven initiatives.

* **53 Gladstone Ave:** Purpose-built live/work creative space designed for immersive content creation, art production, and overnight shoot capabilities.
* **52 St. Lawrence St (Unit 308):** Multi-set production environment featuring distinct, modular aesthetic zones including the *"Green and Gold Studio,"* *"Midcentury Loft,"* and *"Plant Paradise."*
* **77 Florence Street (Unit 301):** Fully equipped functional studio, built out for heavy production lighting and live performance capabilities.
* **192 Spadina Ave (Suites 215, 403, 410):** Multi-unit operational hub (2013–2015) located within the Centre for Social Innovation (CSI) building, deeply integrating the studio with Toronto's tech and social-impact ecosystems.
* **Bellwoods Studio (Tecumseth St):** Additional physical production node integrated into the downtown creative network.
* **Wade Avenue (87 Wade & Brick/Beam space):** Foundational incubator spaces utilized for photography, stop-motion shoots, art critiques, and community hosting.
* **403-90 Ontario St:** Primary operational hub and initial equipment leasing footprint.
* **Flick the Switch:** Tactical, agile workspace node enabling flexible plug-and-play access within the city's broader creative geography.
